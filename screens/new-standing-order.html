How should orders be sent?
Choose whether generated orders go straight to the supplier or land in your drafts for review first.
Auto-draft
Creates a draft order at the scheduled time. You review and approve before it goes to the supplier. Recommended for most standing orders.
Auto-submit
Sends the order directly to the supplier at the scheduled time with no review step. Best for low-value, highly predictable orders.
Auto-submit orders are sent immediately at the scheduled time — no approval required. Make sure quantities are correct before activating, and review the first few runs. Any items with Dynamic mode will still require approval.
