What type of recipe is this?

The type determines how costing, outlet use, and central kitchen production are handled.

Finished recipe

A complete menu item sold directly to customers. Linked to a POS item and has a selling price. FC% and margin are tracked per serve.

POS-linked · Per serve
Preparation recipe

A sub-recipe or base (sauce, dough, stock) used as an ingredient in finished dishes. Costed per batch; its cost rolls up automatically.

Sub-recipe · Per batch
Production batch

Bulk-produced at the central kitchen and distributed to outlets as a stocked item. Reduces outlet inventory when transferred.

Central kitchen · Bulk
