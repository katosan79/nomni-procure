Roles & permissions
Owner
Full access to all venues, billing, and account settings. Cannot be suspended.
Admin
Full access across all venues. Can manage users and venue configuration.
Manager
Can approve orders, manage suppliers, and configure assigned venues.
Buyer
Can create and submit orders for assigned venues. Cannot approve.
Viewer
Read-only access to orders, inventory, and reports for assigned venues.

Invite user

Admins always have access to all venues.
