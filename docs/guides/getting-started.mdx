## 1. Log in

Go to your Nomni URL and enter your email and password. You'll be prompted for a 6-digit MFA code on your second step. Enterprise accounts can use SSO — click **Continue with SSO** and enter your company domain.

## 2. Understand the dashboard

After logging in you'll land on the **Dashboard** — your HQ morning brief. The top strip shows one card per AI agent with their pending suggestions. Below that: key metrics, outlets needing attention, and a live activity feed.

<Tip>
If you're an Outlet Manager, your dashboard is scoped to your outlet only. You won't see the cross-outlet KPIs.
</Tip>

## 3. Check your outlets are set up

Before raising any orders, confirm your outlets exist and have suppliers linked.

1. Go to **Outlets** in the left nav
2. Click into your outlet and verify the **Suppliers** tab shows at least one linked supplier
3. If suppliers are missing, ask your HQ Admin to link them via the market list

## 4. Raise your first order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Orders">
    Click **Orders** in the left nav, then click **New order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an outlet and supplier">
    Choose the outlet you're ordering for and the supplier you want to order from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add items">
    Items that have a par level set will appear pre-filled with a suggested quantity. Adjust as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Check the order total and delivery date, then click **Submit order**. If your outlet group requires HQ approval, the order will go into a Pending Approval state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 5. Receive the delivery

When goods arrive, open the order from **Orders → [your order]** and click **Receive delivery**. Record actual quantities received. Any shortfalls are flagged automatically as a discrepancy.

## 6. Match the invoice

Once you receive a supplier invoice, go to **Invoices** and either upload it (the system will OCR the data) or enter it manually. The system will attempt a 3-way match against the purchase order and goods receipt automatically.
