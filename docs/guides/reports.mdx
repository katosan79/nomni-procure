The Reporting module gives HQ a single view of financial performance across all outlets. Finance users and HQ Admins have full access; Outlet Managers see their own outlet's data only.

## What's in the report

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Revenue & food cost** | Revenue vs food cost % over time, by outlet or group |
| **Top spend items** | Ranked list of highest-cost ingredients across the group |
| **Supplier spend** | Total spend per supplier, trend over the period |
| **Variance summary** | Actual vs theoretical usage by item and outlet |
| **Order activity** | Orders raised, value, approval time |
| **Invoice reconciliation** | Matched vs unmatched invoices, outstanding amounts |

## Filtering

All charts and tables respond to the same filter set:

- **Date range** — preset (This week / This month / Last month / Custom)
- **Outlet group** — all groups or a specific one
- **Outlet** — all outlets or a specific one
- **Category** — food, beverage, or supply category

Changing any filter updates every section on the page simultaneously.

## Exporting

Click **Export** on any section to download a CSV or Excel file of the underlying data. The export respects the current filter state — what you see is what you export.

## Benchmarking

In HQ Mode, the reporting screen can benchmark outlets against each other. An outlet at 34% food cost when the group average is 27% is a signal to investigate — check their GRN accuracy, count quality, and waste records.

<Info>
Benchmarking requires at least 4 weeks of complete data (GRNs, stock counts, POS mappings) before the numbers are meaningful.
</Info>

## Data completeness checklist

At the top of the reporting screen, a checklist shows data quality signals:

- **POS mapping coverage** — % of POS items mapped to recipes
- **GRN completeness** — orders with a confirmed GRN vs total orders
- **Stock count frequency** — last count date per outlet
- **Invoice reconciliation** — unmatched invoice lines

Low scores on any of these will affect the accuracy of every metric on the page.
