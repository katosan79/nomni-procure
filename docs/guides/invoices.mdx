title: Invoices & 3-Way Match
description: Process supplier invoices and reconcile them against orders and deliveries.
---

Nomni's invoice module handles the full accounts payable flow: ingest the invoice, extract the data, match it against the purchase order and GRN, and export to your accounting system.

## How invoices get in

Invoices can enter Nomni in three ways:

| Method | How |
|---|---|
| **Email / upload** | Forward supplier invoices to your Nomni inbox or upload a PDF manually. The Invoice Agent extracts line items via OCR. |
| **Supplier e-invoice** | Suppliers using Nomni Supply submit invoices electronically — no OCR needed, data is structured. |
| **PEPPOL** | For suppliers on the PEPPOL network, invoices arrive automatically via EDI. |

## The 3-way match

For every invoice, Nomni attempts to match three documents:

1. **Purchase Order** — what you ordered and at what price
2. **Goods Receipt Note** — what you actually received
3. **Invoice** — what the supplier is charging

If all three align within tolerance, the invoice is **auto-matched** and moves to Ready for payment. If there's a discrepancy, it's flagged for Finance review.

### Match outcomes

| Result | What it means | Action required |
|---|---|---|
| **Matched** | PO, GRN, and invoice agree | Export to accounting |
| **Price variance** | Invoice price differs from PO price | Finance approves or disputes |
| **Quantity variance** | Invoice qty differs from GRN qty | Finance approves or raises credit note |
| **No PO found** | Invoice arrived with no matching order | Finance creates a manual PO or rejects |

## Exporting to accounting

Once matched and approved, invoices can be exported to your accounting system. Currently supported: **Xero**. The export creates a bill in Xero with full line-item detail, supplier mapping, and GL codes.

<Info>
GL code mapping is configured in **Settings → Accounting**. Contact your HQ Admin if codes are missing for certain item categories.
</Info>

## Invoice statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Processing | OCR extraction in progress |
| Needs review | Extracted data needs human verification |
| Matched | 3-way match passed |
| Partially matched | Some lines matched, others need attention |
| Disputed | Finance raised a dispute with supplier |
| Approved | Approved for payment |
| Paid | Marked as paid, exported to accounting |
| Rejected | Voided, will not be paid |
