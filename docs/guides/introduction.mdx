---
title: What is Nomni Procure?
description: A procurement and inventory platform built for multi-outlet hospitality groups.
---

Nomni Procure covers the full procure-to-pay cycle for hospitality operators — from automated replenishment through purchase orders, goods receipt, invoice matching, and accounting export. It also handles recipe costing, stock counting, POS menu mapping, and cross-outlet reporting.

## Who it's for

Nomni is built for groups running multiple outlets — cafes, restaurants, hotels, and quick-service brands that manage shared suppliers, centralised menus, and distributed teams.

| If you are… | You'll primarily use… |
|---|---|
| HQ Administrator | Market lists, recipes, POS mapping, users, reporting |
| HQ Approver | Order approvals, recipe sign-off, POS mapping review |
| Outlet Manager | Raising orders, receiving goods, stock counts |
| Outlet User | Raising orders, receiving goods, counting stock |
| Finance User | Invoice management, 3-way match, Xero export |
| Supplier | Nomni Supply portal — view orders, submit e-invoices |

## How it's structured

Nomni organises everything around a hierarchy:

```
HQ → Brand → Region → Outlet Group → Outlet
```

HQ sets the rules (pricing, recipes, POS mappings). Outlets operate within those rules. The platform enforces the boundary so your teams stay in sync without needing to email spreadsheets back and forth.

## Operating modes

Your outlet group runs in one of three modes, set during onboarding:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="HQ Mode" icon="building">
    All market lists, recipes, and POS mappings governed centrally. Outlets have read-only views.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hybrid" icon="arrows-split-up-and-left">
    Outlets raise orders and run counts. HQ controls pricing and recipes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Standalone" icon="store">
    Individual outlet operates independently — useful for franchisees or pilot sites.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
